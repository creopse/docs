# Overview

This is the first page you see when logging into the administration. It gives you a quick snapshot of your site's activity without having to browse through each section.

![Overview](/images/screenshots/en/light/dashboard.png#light-only)
![Overview](/images/screenshots/en/dark/dashboard.png#dark-only)

## Email verification

When your account is created, a verification link is sent by email. Until it's clicked, a banner reminds you at the top of the page — check your spam folder if you can't find it.

## Getting started

A block of shortcuts to the most useful actions when starting out: page sections, content models, content permalinks, and administration settings.

## Site identity

A summary of your site (name, phone, email, address, postal code) with an **Edit** button leading directly to [Content Management](./content-management).

## Statistics

- **Players** — number of administrators and other users with access to the administration.
- **News** — number of articles, categories, tags, and comments.
- **Website Visits** — total number of visits and their geographic distribution on a world map.
