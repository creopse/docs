# Visual Editor

The visual editor lets you edit a page's content directly on its rendering, rather than through the [Content Management](./content-management) forms. Your changes only affect the content — never the design of the sections.

![Visual Editor](/images/screenshots/en/light/visual-editor.png#light-only)
![Visual Editor](/images/screenshots/en/dark/visual-editor.png#dark-only)

## Finding your way around

- The selector at the top lets you switch pages without leaving the editor.
- The device icons (desktop, laptop, tablet, mobile) preview the rendering for each screen size.
- **Publish** makes your changes publicly visible; **Preview** opens a preview without publishing.
- The left panel lists the page's sections — clicking a section (in the list or directly in the preview) selects it for editing.
- **Insert or remove sections** adds or removes a section without leaving the editor.

At the bottom of the screen: the number of sections on the page, and the time of the last save.

::: tip
Remember to publish once your changes are complete — until you do, only the previous version stays visible on the public site.
:::
