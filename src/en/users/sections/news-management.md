# News Management

Create and publish your site's news: articles, categories, tags, and visitor comments.

![News Management](/images/screenshots/en/light/news-management.png#light-only)
![News Management](/images/screenshots/en/dark/news-management.png#dark-only)

## Articles

The **Add article** button opens the creation form (title, content, cover image, publication date...). The list can be filtered by month, tag, or category, and by status through the **All / My Articles / Draft / Pending / Published** tabs.

## Comments

Comments left by visitors on your articles are managed from the **Comments** tab in the side menu (moderation, deletion).

## Categories and tags

**Categories** and **Tags** let you organize your articles to make them easier to browse and find on the public site.
