# Account Information

View and update your personal information: last name, first name(s), username, email address, password, phone number, address, and geographic location.

![Account Information](/images/screenshots/en/light/account-information.png#light-only)
![Account Information](/images/screenshots/en/dark/account-information.png#dark-only)

Sensitive fields (username, email, password) are locked by default — click the pencil icon next to a field to edit it. The **Roles** block at the bottom of the page reminds you of your current role (see [Access management](./access-management)) and cannot be changed from this page. Don't forget to click **Save** after any change.
