# Content Management

This section brings together everything related to your site's public content: its general information, its content models (services, projects...), and its pages.

## Basic information

Website name, email, phone, address, postal code, and description — the information displayed across your site (Header, Footer, contact pages...). Neighboring tabs let you manage branding (logo, colors) and social networks.

![Basic Information](/images/screenshots/en/light/basic-information.png#light-only)
![Basic Information](/images/screenshots/en/dark/basic-information.png#dark-only)

## Content models

A content model represents a repeating type of content specific to your site — for example your services or projects — with a defined structure (title, description, image...) and a list of entries. The **Editorial content / User entries / System data** tabs filter models by origin, and **Open** gives access to a model's entries to create, edit, or delete them.

![Content Models](/images/screenshots/en/light/content-models.png#light-only)
![Content Models](/images/screenshots/en/dark/content-models.png#dark-only)

## Pages

The list on the left shows all of your site's pages (search, add, reorder). Selecting a page reveals:

- its sections, in display order, each with a **Display** checkbox to publish or hide it without deleting it;
- a **Data & Settings** button per section to edit its content;
- **Insert or remove sections** to compose the page;
- **Open the visual editor** to edit the content directly on the page's rendering — see [Visual Editor](./visual-editor).
