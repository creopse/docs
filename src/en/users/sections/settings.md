# Settings

Configure your site's general options, organized into four tabs: **General**, **Appearance**, **Content**, and **Translation**.

![Settings](/images/screenshots/en/light/settings.png#light-only)
![Settings](/images/screenshots/en/dark/settings.png#dark-only)

## Appearance

Graphic elements of the administration (logo, icon, and their monochrome versions) and customization of the authentication pages (background image and color, for both light and dark mode).

## Global actions

- **Export** downloads a copy of your current settings.
- **Import** restores settings from a previously exported file.
- **Restore** reverts to the default values.
- **Save** applies the current changes.

::: tip
Export your settings before making a significant change — this lets you roll back easily if something goes wrong.
:::
