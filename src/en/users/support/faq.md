# FAQ

Common questions about using the Creopse administration. If your question isn't here, contact your administrator.

## Content & publishing

### I edited a section but nothing changed on the public site

You probably missed the publishing step: in the [Visual Editor](../sections/visual-editor), changes only become publicly visible after clicking **Publish**. The **Preview** button lets you check the rendering before publishing.

### How do I add a new page or reorder its sections?

From [Content Management](../sections/content-management), the page list offers **Add page** and **Rearrange pages**. Once a page is selected, its sections can be reordered, shown/hidden, or edited directly.

### I can't find how to attach a new section to a page

From the page in question (in [Content Management](../sections/content-management)), the **Insert or remove sections** block lets you add one. The same action is also available from the [Visual Editor](../sections/visual-editor).

### What's the difference between an article's statuses (Draft, Pending, Published)?

**Draft** is only visible to its author, **Pending** flags an article that's ready but awaiting approval, and **Published** is visible on the public site. See [News Management](../sections/news-management).

### A section's content isn't showing in a given language

Check that the corresponding field was actually filled in for that language — Creopse content is bilingual per field, a missing value in one language stays empty rather than automatically falling back to the other language.

## Media

### I deleted a file by mistake, can it be recovered?

Yes, as long as it hasn't been permanently deleted: the **Trash** tab in the [Media Library](../sections/media-library) groups deleted files and lets you restore them.

## Users & access

### How do I add a new administrator?

From [User Management](../sections/user-management), the **Add** button creates a new account, which is then assigned a role from [Access management](../sections/access-management).

### I don't have access to a section I should be able to see

This depends on the permissions tied to your role. Ask an administrator to check your role in [Access management](../sections/access-management), or check your current role from [Account Information](../sections/account-info).

## Account

### I forgot my password

Use the recovery link on the login page. Once logged in, the password can be changed at any time from [Account Information](../sections/account-info) (click the pencil icon next to the **Password** field).

### I didn't receive my account's verification email

Check your spam folder — the banner shown on the [Overview](../sections/dashboard) page also lets you resend the verification link.

## Extensions

### Is it safe to install a plugin?

A plugin has access to your site once installed — only install plugins from a source you trust. See [Extensions](../sections/plugins).
