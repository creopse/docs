# Newsletter

This section manages the site's newsletter subscribers (by email and by phone) as well as the campaigns sent out, across three tabs.

## Newsletter Emails

![Newsletter Emails](/images/screenshots/en/light/newsletter-emails.png#light-only)
![Newsletter Emails](/images/screenshots/en/dark/newsletter-emails.png#dark-only)

List of subscribed email addresses (**Email**, **Creation** columns). **Add an email address** registers one manually; **Export**/**Import** manage the list in bulk through a file.

## Newsletter Numbers

![Newsletter Numbers](/images/screenshots/en/light/newsletter-numbers.png#light-only)
![Newsletter Numbers](/images/screenshots/en/dark/newsletter-numbers.png#dark-only)

Same principle as emails, for phone number subscribers.

## Newsletter Campaigns

![Newsletter Campaigns](/images/screenshots/en/light/newsletter-campaigns.png#light-only)
![Newsletter Campaigns](/images/screenshots/en/dark/newsletter-campaigns.png#dark-only)

List of sent campaigns (**Title**, **Subject** columns). **Add a campaign** creates a new one.
