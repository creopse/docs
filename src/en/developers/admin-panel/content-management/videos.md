# Videos

This section manages the site's videos, whether self-hosted or imported from YouTube.

![Videos](/images/screenshots/en/light/videos.png#light-only)
![Videos](/images/screenshots/en/dark/videos.png#dark-only)

## Organization

- The **All / Self-hosted / YouTube** tabs filter by source.
- **Categories** classifies videos by category.
- **Settings** groups the general settings for this section.
- The **Search video** field finds a video by name.

## Actions

**Add video** opens the add form (self-hosted file or YouTube URL depending on the source). The **Select all** checkbox lets you apply an action to several videos at once.
